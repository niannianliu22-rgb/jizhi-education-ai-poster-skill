## Placement rules

- Prefer the listed transparent PNG where available for a free-standing composition.
- Preserve aspect ratio. Do not stretch, repaint, regenerate, or replace chest marks.
- Never leave an accidental square or rectangular source background visibly layered over the poster background.
- If only a background-bearing source is available, use the complete source as a deliberate framed card or omit it.
- Case evidence is not bundled here. It must be supplied separately for each case and kept original under the case-evidence rules.
